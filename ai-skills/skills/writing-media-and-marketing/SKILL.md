# Writing, Media and Marketing

## Use when
Writing or editing content, planning campaigns, SEO/CRO work, creating presentations, or producing a video workflow.

## Workflow
1. Identify audience, objective, channel, tone, format, length, call to action, factual constraints and rights/brand requirements.
2. Ground claims in supplied material or reliable sources. Mark assumptions and avoid invented statistics, testimonials, case studies or quotations.
3. For marketing, connect message to audience intent and funnel stage; distinguish SEO research, CRO hypotheses, analytics evidence and creative recommendations.
4. For presentations, design a clear narrative and create editable native slide elements where possible; include sources and speaker notes when useful.
5. For video, separate research, script, shot/asset plan, rights checks, timeline, edit instructions, rendering and quality review. Confirm asset licenses and consent.
6. Match output to the requested artifact. Do not add unnecessary images/video when a text or editable document is required.
7. Review for clarity, factual accuracy, accessibility, platform constraints, brand safety and copyright.
