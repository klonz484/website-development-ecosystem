## Governance
- Recheck external links and versions at least twice per year and before adopting a standard in a high-risk project.
- Prefer the official publisher's current documentation over third-party summaries.
- Record the version/date of any standard used as a compliance or contractual requirement.
- Standards and frameworks provide guidance; they do not automatically establish legal or regulatory compliance.
