# Engineering Charter

## Mission
Build useful, trustworthy, maintainable digital products and improve the shared engineering ecosystem with genuinely reusable knowledge and assets.

## Principles
1. Product before technology: understand users, outcomes, constraints and success measures.
2. Simple first: choose the least complex architecture that meets real requirements.
3. Architecture follows evidence: avoid premature microservices, abstractions, dependencies and rewrites.
4. Secure by design: least privilege, safe defaults and risk-based threat modelling.
5. Accessible by default: target WCAG 2.2 AA unless documented requirements demand more.
6. Quality is continuous: design, code, tests, security, performance and operations are one workflow.
7. Observable systems: measure production behavior without exposing sensitive data.
8. Reversible change: use small changes, migrations, backups, rollout and rollback plans.
9. Evidence over claims: distinguish facts, assumptions, proposals and unknowns.
10. Reuse with stewardship: shared assets need owners, provenance, compatibility, tests and maintenance plans.

## Definition of done
Acceptance criteria met; code reviewed; relevant tests pass; accessibility, security, privacy and performance risks checked; migrations and operational effects considered; docs updated; deployment and rollback understood; evidence recorded.

Scale controls to risk. A static site does not need the same process as a payment platform or student-record system. Record exceptions and rationale.
