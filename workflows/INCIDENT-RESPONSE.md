# Incident Response

1. Detect and confirm the issue; assess user impact and scope.
2. Declare an incident lead and technical owner; establish communications.
3. Contain safely: disable affected features, revoke exposed credentials, isolate systems or roll back.
4. Investigate while preserving evidence and limiting access to sensitive data.
5. Recover; validate data integrity, smoke-test and monitor recurrence.
6. Communicate timely, factual updates; avoid speculation.
7. Write a blameless postmortem: impact, timeline, causes, contributors, detection, response and prevention.
8. Assign owners and due dates; verify corrective actions.

Never publish secrets or personal data in incident reports. Follow applicable contractual and legal notification requirements with qualified advice.
